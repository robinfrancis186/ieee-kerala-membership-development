06Dues and rebate

There is no fixed dues waterfall.

IEEE dues are not divided into a guaranteed sequence of amounts for your Student Branch, Section, Council, and Region. The rules use a shared allocation pool and separate eligibility-based rebates.

2025 MGA Operations Manual
  1. 01Core IEEE dues

    IEEE Bylaws distinguish core IEEE dues from optional, member-choice dues for technical Societies. Society add-ons are not included in this estimate.

  2. 0212% MGA allocation

    The current MGA manual allocates 12% of member dues to Regions, Sections, Chapters, Affinity Groups, and Student Branches as a whole.

  3. 03Formula and eligibility

    Region allocations use prior-year membership, number of Sections, and relative travel cost. Other geographic units use their own rebate schedule and reporting rules.

  4. 04Local unit receives and distributes

    Subunit rebates, including Student Branch rebates, are disbursed through the Section's bank account. Sections and Geographic Councils manage or transfer subunit funds under MGA rules.

The 12% is an aggregate allocation, not the share for any one member's Branch or Section. A Student Branch rebate is a separate branch-level calculation, not a percentage of the member's individual payment. See IEEE Bylaw I-108 and the MGA allocation rules.

07Funding and awards

Find the right funding.

IEEE funds individuals and units. Students and graduate students can win scholarships, travel grants, and awards. Branches, chapters, and Sections can apply for event, project, and activity funding.

IEEE Students opportunities

For individuals

Students and graduate students

  • Scholarships and fellowships, mostly from Societies
  • Student travel grants, many supported by the IEEE Foundation
  • Student project grants from Societies and HTB

For units

Branches, chapters, and Sections

  • Student Branch rebate and SPAx event funding
  • Society chapter funds for activities and growth
  • Event funds from MGA, Region committees, and Societies

08Humanitarian work

Technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE does more than fulfil needs; it improves lives. These programmes fund and support members who apply engineering to community problems.

IEEE doing good

EPICS in IEEE

Funding, mentorship, and visibility for engineering projects in access and abilities, human services, environment, and education. The October 2026 call funds selected projects with up to US$10,000.

epics.ieee.org

IEEE SIGHT

A global network of volunteers partnering with underserved communities on sustainable technology solutions. Kerala Section has its own SIGHT group.

sight.ieee.org

Humanitarian Technologies Board

Tech4Good project grants of US$2,500 to US$10,000 for grassroots projects led by IEEE members (2026 call), plus event sponsorship.

htb.ieee.org

IEEE Foundation

Invests in IEEE unit programmes expected to create measurable, lasting impact. Units can submit funding proposals at any time.

ieeefoundation.org

EPICS and Tech4Good amounts are from their 2026 calls, checked on 14 October 2026. Check each programme’s site for the current call.
